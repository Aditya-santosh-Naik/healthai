## Staying hydrated during illness

Fever, vomiting, diarrhoea and sweating all increase fluid loss. Water, oral
rehydration salts, coconut water, thin dal water, buttermilk and clear soups
are all suitable.

## A practical measure

Urine colour and how often it is passed are more reliable guides than counting
glasses. Pale urine passed regularly suggests adequate intake; dark urine
passed rarely suggests the opposite.

## When to be concerned

Passing very little urine, dizziness on standing, a very dry mouth, or unusual
drowsiness indicate significant dehydration and need medical attention.
