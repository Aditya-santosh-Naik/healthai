## Typical symptoms

Watery diarrhoea, vomiting, stomach cramps, nausea, and sometimes a low-grade
fever, headache and body aches.

## What is not typical

Blood in the stools, a high fever, or severe localised abdominal pain suggest
something other than simple viral gastroenteritis and need medical assessment.
