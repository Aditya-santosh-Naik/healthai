## Why antibiotics are not always the answer

Antibiotics work against bacteria and have no effect on viruses. Colds, flu,
COVID-19 and most sore throats and coughs are viral, so antibiotics neither
help nor shorten them.

## Antimicrobial resistance

Taking antibiotics when they are not needed, or not finishing a prescribed
course, makes bacteria harder to treat in future. India carries a high burden
of antibiotic-resistant infections.

## The practical rule

Antibiotics should be taken only when prescribed by a doctor for a specific
diagnosis, and the course should be completed as directed. Leftover antibiotics
from a previous illness should never be reused.
