## Making a consultation more useful

Doctors work with limited time. Arriving with a clear account of the problem
improves what you get out of the visit.

## What is worth having ready

When the symptoms started and whether they are improving or worsening. Which
symptoms came first. What makes them better or worse. Every medicine currently
being taken, including anything bought without prescription. Known allergies
and what reaction they cause. Existing medical conditions.

Bringing the actual medicine packets, or a photograph of them, is more reliable
than trying to recall names.
