## Helping the sinuses drain

Steam inhalation, warm compresses over the face, and saline nasal rinses are
the most useful self-care measures. Sleeping with the head raised can reduce
overnight congestion.

Staying well hydrated keeps secretions thinner and easier to clear.

## What to avoid

Prolonged use of decongestant nasal sprays can worsen congestion when stopped.
Any nasal spray used for more than a few days should be discussed with a
pharmacist.
