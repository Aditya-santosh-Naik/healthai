## Warning signs of severe dengue

Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding from the gums or nose,
blood in vomit or stools, rapid breathing, extreme tiredness or restlessness.

## The critical timing

These signs typically appear 24 to 48 hours AFTER the fever starts to come
down. Feeling worse as the fever settles is the opposite of reassuring in
dengue, and is the point at which hospital assessment is needed urgently.
