## Emergency signs

Difficulty breathing, persistent pain or pressure in the chest, new confusion,
inability to stay awake, and pale, grey, or blue-tinted skin, lips or nail beds
all require emergency care immediately.

## Monitoring at home

Breathlessness that worsens over hours rather than days is a particular
concern. If a pulse oximeter is available, a falling oxygen reading warrants
urgent assessment even if the person feels reasonably well.
