## When a cold needs a doctor

See a doctor if symptoms last beyond ten days, if they improve and then clearly
worsen again, or if a high fever develops. Difficulty breathing, chest pain,
or wheezing should be assessed promptly rather than waited out.

People with asthma, diabetes, heart disease, or a weakened immune system should
seek advice earlier rather than later.
