## Oral rehydration salts

Oral rehydration salts, sold as Electral and ORS sachets, replace the water and
salts lost through diarrhoea, vomiting, fever or heavy sweating. They are
available without prescription and are one of the most effective simple
treatments in medicine.

## Using them

Sachets are mixed with the volume of clean water stated on the packet. Using
less water than stated makes the solution too concentrated, which can worsen
dehydration. Follow the packet instructions or ask a pharmacist.

Homemade sugar-salt solutions are a fallback when sachets are unavailable, but
prepared sachets are more reliable.
