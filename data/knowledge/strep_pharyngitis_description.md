## What strep throat is

Strep throat is a bacterial throat infection caused by group A Streptococcus.
Unlike most sore throats, which are viral, this one responds to antibiotics.

## Why it matters

Untreated strep throat can occasionally lead to complications including
rheumatic fever, which can affect the heart. This is why confirming it and
treating it properly matters more than for an ordinary sore throat.
