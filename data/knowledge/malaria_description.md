## What malaria is

Malaria is caused by Plasmodium parasites transmitted through the bite of
infected female Anopheles mosquitoes, which bite mainly between dusk and dawn.

## Why prompt diagnosis matters

Some forms of malaria can progress rapidly to severe illness. Any fever in
someone who has been in a malaria-endemic area should be tested promptly rather
than observed.
