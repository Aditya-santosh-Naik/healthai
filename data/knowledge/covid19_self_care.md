## Managing mild illness at home

Rest, fluids, and monitoring breathing are the core of home care. Isolating
from other household members as far as practical reduces spread.

## Positioning

Lying on the front or side rather than flat on the back can make breathing more
comfortable for some people. This does not replace medical assessment if
breathing is difficult.
