## Symptoms that need investigation

Difficulty or pain on swallowing, food sticking, unintentional weight loss,
persistent vomiting, black stools, or vomiting blood all need prompt medical
assessment and usually an endoscopy.

## Do not assume chest pain is reflux

Reflux and cardiac pain can feel similar. Chest pain that comes with sweating,
breathlessness, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw must be treated as a
possible heart problem until a doctor says otherwise.
