## Anti-inflammatory painkillers

Ibuprofen, diclofenac, naproxen and aspirin belong to a group called NSAIDs.
Combiflam and Brufen are common Indian brands containing ibuprofen.

## Who needs to be careful

These medicines can irritate the stomach lining, raise blood pressure, and
affect kidney function. They need particular care in people with high blood
pressure, kidney disease, acid reflux, gastritis or ulcers, asthma, or who take
blood thinners.

They should also be avoided in suspected dengue because of bleeding risk.

Anyone in these groups should ask a doctor or pharmacist before using them.
