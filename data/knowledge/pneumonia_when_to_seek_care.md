## Seek care immediately

Increasing breathlessness, chest pain, confusion, bluish lips or fingertips, or
an inability to speak in full sentences all require emergency attention.

## Seek care the same day

A cough with fever and breathlessness should be assessed by a doctor promptly.
Pneumonia is diagnosed with examination and usually a chest X-ray, neither of
which can be done at home.
