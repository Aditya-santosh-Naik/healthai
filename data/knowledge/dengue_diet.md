## Fluids are the priority

Maintaining fluid intake matters more than any particular food in dengue. Oral
rehydration salts, coconut water, thin dal water, and plain water are all
suitable. Passing pale urine regularly is a practical sign of adequate intake.

## Eating during illness

Soft, light food such as khichdi, curd rice, banana and upma is easier to
manage when nauseated. Papaya and pomegranate are commonly taken in India;
they are harmless, though they are not a treatment.
