## Seek care if

There is blood in the stools, a high fever, severe abdominal pain, signs of
dehydration, or symptoms lasting more than three days.

## A caution about anti-diarrhoeal medicines

Medicines that stop diarrhoea can prolong some bacterial infections by keeping
the organism in the gut. They should not be used when there is fever or blood
in the stool without medical advice.
