## What dengue is

Dengue is a viral infection spread by Aedes mosquitoes, which bite mainly
during daylight hours. It is common across India, particularly during and after
the monsoon.

## Phases of illness

Dengue typically has a febrile phase of two to seven days, followed by a
critical phase around the time the fever drops. Most people recover fully, but
a minority develop severe dengue during that critical phase.
