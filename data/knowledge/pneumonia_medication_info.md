## This needs a doctor

Bacterial pneumonia is treated with prescription antibiotics, and the choice
depends on the likely organism, the person's age, and their other conditions.
This is not something to self-treat or to manage with leftover antibiotics.

Viral pneumonia does not respond to antibiotics and is managed differently.

## Completing treatment

If a doctor prescribes a course, it should be completed even once symptoms
improve. Stopping early risks the infection returning.
