## Typical flu symptoms

Fever or feeling feverish with chills, muscle and body aches, headache,
tiredness, and a dry cough are characteristic. A sore throat and runny nose can
occur but are usually less prominent than in a cold.

Not everyone with flu develops a fever.

## Timing

Symptoms usually last three to seven days, though cough and tiredness can
continue for two weeks or more.
