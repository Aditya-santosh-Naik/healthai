## Features suggesting strep

Sudden severe sore throat, pain on swallowing, fever, white patches or streaks
of pus on the tonsils, and tender swollen glands in the neck. Tiny red spots on
the roof of the mouth may be present.

## Features suggesting it is NOT strep

Cough, runny nose, hoarseness, and conjunctivitis point toward a viral cause
rather than strep. Their presence makes strep considerably less likely.
