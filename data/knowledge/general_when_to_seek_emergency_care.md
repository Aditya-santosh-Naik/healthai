## Signs that always mean emergency care

Chest pain, especially spreading to the arm, jaw or back, or with sweating or
breathlessness. Severe difficulty breathing or blue lips. Sudden weakness on
one side of the body, facial drooping, or slurred speech. Seizures. Fainting or
loss of consciousness. Heavy or uncontrolled bleeding. Confusion of sudden
onset.

## In India

The national emergency number is 112. Ambulance services can also be reached on
108 in most states.

Do not wait to see whether these settle on their own, and do not use a symptom
checker to decide.
