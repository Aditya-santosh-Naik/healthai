## Typical symptoms

Fever, chills, and sweating are the classic triad, often occurring in cycles
with the person feeling relatively well in between. Headache, body aches,
nausea, vomiting, and marked tiredness are common.

## Recognising the pattern

The cyclical pattern of shaking chills followed by high fever and then profuse
sweating is characteristic, though it is not always present, particularly early
in the illness.
