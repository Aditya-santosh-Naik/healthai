## Signs of dehydration needing care

Passing very little urine, dark urine, extreme thirst, dry mouth, sunken eyes,
dizziness on standing, and unusual drowsiness.

Urine output is a more reliable guide than how many times someone has opened
their bowels.

## Other reasons to seek care

Blood in the stools, inability to keep any fluids down, severe abdominal pain,
or symptoms lasting beyond three days.
