## What pneumonia is

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs of one or both lungs,
which may fill with fluid. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi.

## Why it is serious

Unlike bronchitis, which affects the airways, pneumonia affects the lung tissue
itself and interferes with oxygen reaching the blood. It is a leading cause of
hospital admission and needs proper medical treatment rather than home
management alone.
