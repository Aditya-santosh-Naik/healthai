## Managing a fever

Fever is the body's response to infection rather than an illness in itself.
Rest, fluids, and light clothing are the mainstays. Cool sponging can help
comfort but does not treat the cause.

## When a fever needs assessment

A fever lasting more than three days without an obvious cause, a very high
fever, a fever that settles and then returns, or fever with confusion, a stiff
neck, difficulty breathing, or a rash that does not fade under pressure all
need medical assessment.

Adults with diabetes, heart or lung disease, kidney disease, or a weakened
immune system should seek advice sooner.
