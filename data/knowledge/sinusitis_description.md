## What sinusitis is

Sinusitis is inflammation of the air-filled spaces around the nose and eyes.
It most often follows a cold, when swelling blocks the sinuses and mucus cannot
drain.

## Viral versus bacterial

Most sinusitis is viral and settles within about ten days. Symptoms lasting
beyond ten days, or worsening after an initial improvement, raise the
possibility of a bacterial infection.
