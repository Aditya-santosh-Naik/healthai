## Typical symptoms

A cough producing phlegm, fever with chills, shortness of breath, and chest
pain that worsens on breathing deeply or coughing. Rapid breathing, tiredness,
and loss of appetite are common.

## In older adults

Older adults may present atypically, with confusion or a lower-than-normal
temperature rather than a clear fever. A sudden change in alertness in an older
person deserves assessment.
