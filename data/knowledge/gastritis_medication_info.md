## Painkillers to be careful with

Anti-inflammatory painkillers including ibuprofen, diclofenac, naproxen and
aspirin irritate the stomach lining and can both cause and worsen gastritis.
Anyone with gastritis taking these regularly should discuss alternatives with a
doctor rather than simply stopping a prescribed medicine.

## Treatment depends on the cause

If Helicobacter pylori is found, it is treated with a specific combination of
prescription medicines. Acid-reducing medicines are also commonly prescribed.
Both require a doctor's assessment, since the right choice depends on the cause.
