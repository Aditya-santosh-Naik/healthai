## What food poisoning is

Food poisoning is illness caused by eating food contaminated with bacteria,
viruses, parasites, or their toxins.

## Timing is the clue

Symptoms often begin within hours of the meal, which is faster than most other
gut infections. If several people who ate the same meal fall ill together, that
strongly suggests a foodborne cause.
