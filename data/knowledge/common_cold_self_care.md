## Looking after yourself

Rest and fluids remain the mainstay. Warm drinks can ease a sore throat, and
steam inhalation can loosen a blocked nose. Gargling with warm salt water helps
some people.

## Avoiding passing it on

Cold viruses spread easily through droplets and hands. Washing hands often,
covering coughs, and using a separate towel reduce spread within a household.
