## What acute bronchitis is

Acute bronchitis is inflammation of the airways that carry air to the lungs. It
usually follows a cold or other respiratory infection and is almost always
caused by a virus.

## Why the cough lasts

The airway lining stays irritated after the infection clears, which is why the
cough commonly continues for two to three weeks. A lingering cough alone does
not mean the infection is ongoing.
