## What a common cold is

A cold is a mild infection of the nose and throat caused by a virus. More than
200 different viruses can cause one, which is why people catch colds
repeatedly. Adults average two to three colds a year.

## How it usually goes

Symptoms normally appear one to three days after exposure. They tend to build
over the first two or three days and then settle. Most colds clear in seven to
ten days, though a cough can linger longer.

Because a cold is viral, antibiotics do not treat it and do not shorten it.
