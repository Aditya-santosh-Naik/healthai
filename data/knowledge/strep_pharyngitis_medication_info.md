## Diagnosis comes before treatment

Strep throat is confirmed with a throat swab or rapid test. Because most sore
throats are viral, treating every sore throat with antibiotics is both
ineffective and harmful.

## If it is confirmed

A doctor will prescribe an antibiotic course, commonly a penicillin-type
medicine. Anyone with a penicillin allergy must tell their doctor, as a
different class is needed.

The full course should be completed even after the throat feels better.
