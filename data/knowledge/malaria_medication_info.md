## Confirm before treating

Malaria treatment depends on which parasite species is involved and on local
resistance patterns. Diagnosis is made with a blood test, and treatment must be
prescribed by a doctor.

Taking antimalarial medicines without a confirmed diagnosis is a significant
driver of drug resistance and can mask a different serious illness.

## Completing treatment

Antimalarial courses must be completed in full. Some forms of malaria also need
a follow-up course to prevent relapse, which a doctor will advise on.
