## Typical symptoms

Nausea, vomiting, stomach cramps and diarrhoea, sometimes with a mild fever
and weakness. Onset is usually rapid, from a couple of hours to a day after
eating.

## Duration

Most cases settle within one to three days once the body clears the cause.
Symptoms persisting beyond that need medical review.
