## Typical presentation

A persistent cough is the dominant symptom, often bringing up clear, yellow or
green mucus. Chest tightness or soreness, mild wheezing, tiredness, and a
low-grade temperature are common.

The cough is frequently worse at night.

## What is not typical

A high fever, breathlessness at rest, or coughing up blood are not expected in
simple bronchitis and should be assessed.
