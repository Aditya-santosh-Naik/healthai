## This requires prescribed antibiotics

Typhoid is a bacterial infection and needs a proper antibiotic course
prescribed by a doctor after the diagnosis is confirmed, usually with a blood
test. It is not an illness to self-treat.

Antibiotic resistance in typhoid is a growing problem in South Asia, which is
why the choice of antibiotic must be a clinician's decision.

## Finishing the course

The full course must be completed even after the fever settles. Stopping early
is a common cause of relapse and contributes to resistance.
