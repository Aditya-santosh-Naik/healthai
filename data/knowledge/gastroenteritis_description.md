## What gastroenteritis is

Gastroenteritis is inflammation of the stomach and intestines, usually caused
by a virus and often called stomach flu, although it is unrelated to
influenza.

## How long it lasts

Most cases settle within one to three days, though some last up to a week.
It spreads very easily between people in a household.
