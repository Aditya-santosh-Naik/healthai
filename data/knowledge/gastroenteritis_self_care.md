## Rehydration is the treatment

Oral rehydration salts after each loose stool are the single most important
measure. Small frequent sips are better tolerated than large volumes, which can
trigger further vomiting.

## Eating again

Once vomiting settles, bland food such as rice, banana, curd rice, khichdi and
toast is usually well tolerated. Milk is often poorly handled for a few days
after a gut infection.

## Preventing spread

Thorough handwashing with soap matters; alcohol gels are less effective against
some of the viruses that cause this.
