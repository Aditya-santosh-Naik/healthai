## What GERD is

Gastro-oesophageal reflux disease occurs when stomach acid repeatedly flows
back into the food pipe. The muscular valve at the top of the stomach relaxes
when it should stay closed.

## Why it recurs

GERD is usually a long-term pattern rather than a one-off illness. Symptoms
often relate closely to meals, meal timing, and body position, which is why
changes to those habits help more than most people expect.
