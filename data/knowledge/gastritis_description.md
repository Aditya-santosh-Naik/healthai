## What gastritis is

Gastritis is inflammation of the stomach lining. It can come on suddenly or
develop gradually over time.

## Common causes

Infection with Helicobacter pylori, regular use of anti-inflammatory
painkillers such as ibuprofen or diclofenac, and alcohol are the most common
causes. Anti-inflammatory painkillers are a frequently overlooked cause worth
reviewing with a doctor.
