## Antibiotics are usually not the answer

Because acute bronchitis is nearly always viral, antibiotics do not help and do
not shorten the cough. Prescribing them unnecessarily contributes to antibiotic
resistance.

A doctor may consider antibiotics in specific circumstances, such as an
underlying lung condition. That judgement belongs to a clinician who can examine
the patient.

## Supportive measures

Fluids and steam help loosen mucus. Stopping smoking matters more than any
medicine here.
