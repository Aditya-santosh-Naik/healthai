## Common symptoms

Fever, dry cough, tiredness, and loss of taste or smell are frequently
reported. Sore throat, headache, body aches, and a blocked or runny nose also
occur.

## The distinguishing feature

Loss of smell or taste, when present, points more strongly toward COVID-19 than
toward a cold or flu. Its absence does not rule COVID-19 out, since many people
never lose these senses.
