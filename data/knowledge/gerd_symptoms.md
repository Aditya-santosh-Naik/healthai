## Typical symptoms

A burning feeling in the chest, often after eating and worse when lying down.
Acid or food coming back up into the throat, a sour taste, and a sensation of
something stuck in the throat.

Hoarseness and a dry night-time cough can also be caused by reflux.

## The distinguishing pattern

Symptoms that worsen lying down and improve with an antacid point strongly
toward reflux rather than a heart or lung cause. Chest pain still needs medical
assessment before being attributed to reflux.
