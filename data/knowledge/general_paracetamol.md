## Paracetamol as general information

Paracetamol, sold in India as Crocin, Dolo and Calpol among others, is widely
available without prescription and is commonly used for fever and pain.

## Why the amount matters

Paracetamol is present in many combination cold and flu products, so it is easy
to take more than intended without realising. Exceeding the safe amount can
cause serious liver damage, and this is a leading cause of acute liver failure.

For that reason, how much to take and how often should be confirmed with a
doctor or pharmacist, who can account for weight, liver health, alcohol intake
and other medicines being taken. This assistant does not give doses.
