## Typical symptoms

Pressure or pain across the cheeks, forehead or around the eyes, a blocked
nose, thick nasal discharge, and mucus dripping down the back of the throat.
Reduced sense of smell, headache, and a mild fever are common.

Pain often worsens on bending forward.
