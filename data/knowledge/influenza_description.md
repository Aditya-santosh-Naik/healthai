## What influenza is

Influenza, or flu, is a respiratory infection caused by influenza viruses. It
is often confused with a cold, but flu tends to come on suddenly and hits
harder.

## How it differs from a cold

A cold builds gradually over days. Flu typically arrives abruptly, with fever,
chills, and aching muscles arriving together. Fatigue after flu can persist for
a couple of weeks even once the fever has settled.
