## Usual symptoms

A runny or blocked nose, sneezing, and a sore throat are the typical opening
symptoms. A cough often follows. Mild tiredness and a low-grade temperature can
occur, particularly in the first days.

## What is less typical

A high fever, severe body aches, or breathlessness are not usual in a simple
cold. Their presence suggests something other than, or in addition to, a cold
and is worth having assessed.
