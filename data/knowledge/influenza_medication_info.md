## What treatment usually involves

Most healthy adults recover from flu with rest and fluids alone. Antibiotics do
not work against influenza because it is a viral illness.

## Prescription antiviral medicines

Antiviral medicines exist and can shorten the illness, but they must be
prescribed by a doctor and generally need to be started within the first day or
two. They are usually reserved for people at higher risk of complications.
Whether they are appropriate is a decision for a doctor, not for self-treatment.
