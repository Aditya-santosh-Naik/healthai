## What COVID-19 is

COVID-19 is a respiratory illness caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Severity
ranges widely: many people have mild illness, while some develop serious
breathing problems.

## Course of illness

Symptoms usually appear five to six days after exposure but can take up to
fourteen days. Most people recover within a few weeks, though some experience
symptoms that persist considerably longer.
