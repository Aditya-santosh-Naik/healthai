## Signs that need urgent care

Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath, persistent pain or pressure in the
chest, ongoing dizziness or confusion, seizures, and not passing urine are all
emergency signs.

## The pattern to watch for

Symptoms that improve and then return with a worse fever or cough can indicate
a secondary bacterial infection such as pneumonia. That pattern warrants
medical assessment.
