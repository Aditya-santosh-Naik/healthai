## Painkillers matter here

There is no specific antiviral treatment for dengue. Care is supportive, with
fluids and rest.

Aspirin, ibuprofen, and other anti-inflammatory painkillers should be avoided
in suspected dengue because they increase the risk of bleeding. Paracetamol is
generally the preferred option for fever and pain, but the choice and amount
should be confirmed with a doctor or pharmacist.

## Monitoring

Doctors often monitor the platelet count during dengue. That monitoring cannot
be done at home.
