## Changes that make the most difference

Avoid eating within three hours of lying down. Eat smaller meals more often
rather than large ones. Raise the head of the bed by six to eight inches using
blocks under the legs, which works better than extra pillows.

Losing even a few kilograms noticeably reduces reflux in people carrying extra
weight.

## Common triggers

Chilli, tamarind, fried and oily food, tea, coffee, citrus, tomato, chocolate,
mint, and carbonated drinks. Alcohol and smoking both relax the valve at the
top of the stomach.
