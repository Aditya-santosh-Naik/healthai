## Seek care promptly

Any fever lasting more than three days without a clear cause should be assessed
by a doctor, particularly after travel or eating outside.

## Emergency signs

Severe abdominal pain, black or bloody stools, or confusion can indicate
intestinal bleeding or perforation. These are surgical emergencies and need
immediate hospital care.
