## Typical symptoms

A gnawing or burning pain in the upper abdomen, indigestion, feeling full after
only a small amount of food, nausea, and bloating.

Pain may improve or worsen with eating depending on the person.

## Some people have no symptoms

Gastritis can be present without any symptoms at all, and is sometimes found
only when an endoscopy is done for another reason.
