## What typhoid is

Typhoid fever is a bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi, spread
through food and water contaminated with the bacteria. It remains common in
parts of South Asia where water and sanitation are inconsistent.

## How it progresses

Symptoms usually begin one to three weeks after exposure and build gradually
rather than suddenly. Untreated, the illness can persist for weeks and cause
serious complications.
