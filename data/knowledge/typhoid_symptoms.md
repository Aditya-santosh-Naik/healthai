## Typical symptoms

A fever that rises steadily day by day, headache, marked weakness and fatigue,
abdominal pain, and loss of appetite. Either constipation or diarrhoea may
occur. Some people develop faint rose-coloured spots on the trunk.

## The distinguishing pattern

The stepwise, gradually climbing fever over a week, together with prominent
weakness and abdominal discomfort, distinguishes typhoid from the abrupt onset
of flu or dengue.
