## Typical symptoms

High fever of sudden onset, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, marked
muscle and joint pain, nausea, vomiting, swollen glands, and a rash.

The intensity of the body pain gives dengue its older name, breakbone fever.

## What argues against dengue

Prominent cough with phlegm, sore throat, and runny nose are not typical of
dengue and point toward a respiratory infection instead.
